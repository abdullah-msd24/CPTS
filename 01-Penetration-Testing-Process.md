# Penetration Testing Process

## What I learned

- Pentests should always be authorized, otherwise it can cause serious issues.
- The person testing should follow a structured path.
- The most important stage is always the information gathering, please focus on this as less info can lead to fraustration and having to come back to this stage.
- Everything you find needs to be documented with proof like screenshots and more, best approach to always have screen recording on when testing.
- The final report should be then shared with the client or organization.
- If you don't' understand anything, it is best to ask client for clarification and be connected with them.

## Pentesting Process

1. Pre-engagement
2. Information gathering
3. Enumeration
4. Vulnerability assessment
5. Exploitation
6. Post-exploitation
7. Reporting

## Important Concepts

### Enumeration

My understanding is that we have to list down the things we find and then find more info about things we find. 

### Exploitation

My understanding is that we have to act upon the info gathered and exploit the target system to get a foothold in the system.

### Reporting

My understanding is that this is the most important part of all the processes as this is the thing that has to be shared with the client or organization and they check it instead of what we do or did. Hence a good report can have a positive impact on client and their team. Report has to be well structured and in depth. It should be like so that a non technical person can unerstand it.
